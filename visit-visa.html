Visit Visa


"Your Destination Is Our Responsibility." Whether for tourism, business meetings, or visiting family, obtaining a visit visa requires careful attention to detail. Our specialist visa officers ensure your application meets all scrutiny criteria.



Check Requirements
There is a stricter scrutiny for visit visas due to a significant movement of the global population seeking economic stability in their lives.

Tourism & Leisure

Explore new destinations and cultures.

Business & Conferencing

Attend meetings, conferences, or seminars.

Family & Social

Reunite with family and friends abroad.

Key Scrutiny Areas

Our officers have identified these 8 key areas that are strictly scrutinized.

1. Valid Passport

Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your stay and have sufficient blank pages.

2. Application Form

Must be accurately and honestly filled out. Errors can lead to immediate rejection.

3. Proof of Funds

Evidence (bank statements, sponsorship) that you can support yourself during the stay.

4. Travel Itinerary

Detailed plans including confirmed flight bookings, accommodation, and activities.

5. Immigration Control

Ensuring you have clear intent to return home and will adhere to visa terms.

6. Economic Concerns

Vetting to ensure you won't become a burden on public resources (healthcare, welfare).

7. Security Risks

Thorough background checks to prevent potential threats or criminal activity.

8. Visa Abuse

Ensuring the visa is not used for unauthorized work or study purposes.

Common Reasons for Refusal

Avoid these pitfalls to ensure a successful application.

  • Inaccurate or fraudulent documentation.
  • Insufficient proof of financial means.
  • Unclear travel plans or lack of return ticket.
  • Perceived risk of overstaying or working illegally.
