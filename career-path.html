Career Path

Take a quick, free Career Test to discover majors and career directions that match your strengths and interests. It is designed for students planning university applications.

What You Get

The Career Test is a fast and insightful way to explore majors that fit your goals. Here is what students can expect.

No Fees

No, the Career Test is completely free of charge. We believe in making career exploration accessible to all students.

05 Minutes

The Career Test takes approximately 15 minutes to complete. It is designed to be a quick and insightful tool for students seeking guidance in choosing a major for their university applications.

Major Insights

The Career Test is tailored to help you identify potential majors that align with your skills and passions. It offers valuable insights to guide your decision-making process.

How It Works

Use the results as a foundation for confident decisions.

01

Answer Honestly

The accuracy of the Career Test depends on the honesty and accuracy of your responses. It is a helpful tool for self-reflection and exploration.

02

Get Matched

Career test results are determined by aggregating scores from your answers, utilizing an algorithm for accuracy, and matching the results with specific careers or fields aligned with your preferences and strengths.

03

Review Your Results

Your Career Test results can be accessed in the "Recommendations" or a designated section on the dashboard. It will provide a summary of your strengths, suggested majors, and recommendations for further exploration.

Career Test FAQ

Answers from the Career Test site to help you get started.

Q1: Are there any fees for taking the Career Test?

No, the Career Test is completely free of charge. We believe in making career exploration accessible to all students.

Q2: What is the estimated completion time for the Career Test?

The Career Test takes approximately 15 minutes to complete. It is designed to be a quick and insightful tool for students seeking guidance in choosing a major for their university applications.

Q3: Will the Career Test assist me in selecting a specific university major?

Yes, the Career Test is tailored to help you identify potential majors that align with your skills and passions. It offers valuable insights to guide your decision-making process.

Q4: Is the Career Test appropriate for all university majors?

The Career Test is designed to be versatile and can provide insights for a wide range of university majors. However, it's important to consider the results as a starting point for further exploration and discussion with career advisors.

Q5 : Can I retake the Career Test if my preferences change?

Yes, you can retake the Career Test at any time to update your preferences and explore new insights. Your profile will be dynamically adjusted based on your latest responses.

Q6 : How reliable is the Career Test in identifying the ideal major for me?

The accuracy of the Career Test depends on the honesty and accuracy of your responses. It is a helpful tool for self-reflection and exploration, but it's advisable to use the results as a starting point and consult with career advisors for personalized guidance.

Q7 : Where can I locate my Career Test results on the dashboard?

Your Career Test results can be accessed in the "Recommendations" or a designated section on the dashboard. It will provide a summary of your strengths, suggested majors, and recommendations for further exploration.

Q8: How will my test results affect the decision-making process?

Use the results of a career test as a foundation for further research and exploration. Consider your personal preferences, goals, and lifestyle when making decisions about your academic and career path.

Q9: How do career tests work?

Career test results are determined by aggregating scores from your answers, utilizing an algorithm for accuracy, and matching the results with specific careers or fields aligned with your preferences and strengths.
