Key Application Requirements

Our specialist visa officers have identified the following key areas that are generally scrutinized during the application process:

01. Valid Passport

Must typically have at least 6 months of validity remaining.

02. Application Form

Accurate, honest, and complete submission of all fields.

03. Financial Means

Bank statements and proof of income to cover all expenses.

04. Travel Itinerary

Detailed flight bookings, accommodation, and activity plans.

05. Purpose of Visit

Invitation letters, admission letters, or meeting details.

06. Ties to Home

Proof of employment or property to ensure your return.

07. Insurance

Travel insurance covering medical emergencies.

08. Health

Medical exams and vaccinations as required by the destination.
