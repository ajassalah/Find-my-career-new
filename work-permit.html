Eligibility Criteria

The most common criteria for a successful work visa application.

01

Job Offer from an Employer

A valid job offer or employment contract from a sponsoring employer is typically the primary requirement.

02

Skills and Qualifications

Evidence of relevant educational qualifications and professional experience to demonstrate your capability for the role.

03

Financial Stability

You must provide proof of sufficient financial resources (Bank Statements) to support yourself during your initial stay.

04

Language Proficiency

Proof of proficiency in the host country’s official language(s) (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL) may be required.

05

Legal & Health Requirements

Clean criminal record (Police Clearance) and satisfactory medical examination reports are mandatory.

06

Labor Market Impact

In some regions, it must be proven that hiring you will not adversely affect the local labor market (e.g. LMIA).
